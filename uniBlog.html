The faculty's learning environment includes smart classrooms, computer laboratories, research and final-year projects, workshops, internships and industry exposure.

From Logic Gates to Robotics

Practical work progresses from verifying Boolean expressions and designing combinational circuits to troubleshooting and advanced semester projects.
